abstract: >-
  Aggregate scores such as final hold-out accuracy hide how LLM memory actually behaves over time.
  SeqMem-Eval is a diagnostic framework that separately measures online utility, hold-out generalization,
  backward transfer, and forgetting, and shows that methods with large accuracy gains often suffer
  substantial forgetting or negative transfer.
